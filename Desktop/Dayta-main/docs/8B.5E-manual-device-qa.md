# 8B.5E Manual Device QA

Status: pending. Static implementation does not constitute device acceptance.

Use two or more accounts and an occurrence materialized by the normal 8B.5D
runtime. Do not run a production backfill solely for this matrix.

## Android

- [ ] Create and edit a routine with zero, one, and multiple participants.
- [ ] Open today's occurrence from Routines, the group dashboard, and Calendar.
- [ ] Confirm generated calendar events remain read-only.
- [ ] Complete, skip, reopen, add a note, edit a note, and retry after a failure.
- [ ] Confirm a viewer and an unassigned member are read-only.
- [ ] As owner/admin/moderator, correct each participant status and excuse one.
- [ ] Complete, skip, and reopen a no-participant occurrence as a manager.
- [ ] Check in concurrently from two devices/accounts; verify counts and rollup.
- [ ] Queue a check-in offline, reconnect, and verify authoritative state.
- [ ] Remove the member before offline replay; verify the replay is rejected.
- [ ] Pause and archive the routine; verify cancellation is read-only and history remains.
- [ ] Background/foreground during a mutation and after a remote correction.
- [ ] Rotate phone and tablet; test narrow/large phones and accessibility text.
- [ ] Test 30 participants and 50 historical occurrences for obvious UI stalls.

## iOS

Repeat the Android matrix and explicitly check:

- [ ] Safe-area layout.
- [ ] Keyboard avoidance with a 500-character note.
- [ ] Background/foreground listener refresh.
- [ ] Portrait and landscape.
- [ ] iPhone and iPad layout.

## Acceptance evidence

Record app build/commit, OS/device, accounts/roles, occurrence IDs, observed
results, and screenshots for failures. Notification deep-link rows remain
pending until the trusted, idempotent backend transition handler is deployed
and observed in a separately approved release.

## Trusted transition side effects

- [ ] Complete, skip, and reopen; verify exactly one activity item per revision.
- [ ] Perform a manager correction and excuse; verify exactly one admin-history row.
- [ ] Confirm the actor never receives their own inbox item or push.
- [ ] Mute the group, then disable only Activity; verify each suppresses inbox and push.
- [ ] Remove or demote a recipient before processing; verify no delivery is created.
- [ ] Tap routine inbox items and pushes from foreground, background, and terminated states.
- [ ] Verify every tap opens the exact occurrence detail route.
- [ ] Retry the same transition event; verify no duplicate activity, inbox, log, or push.
- [ ] Confirm notification text never exposes participant notes or email addresses.

## Final multi-device concurrency

- [ ] Two assigned participants complete simultaneously.
- [ ] One participant completes while another skips.
- [ ] A participant completes while a manager excuses them.
- [ ] Two managers correct the same participant simultaneously.
- [ ] Reopen a resolved occurrence while another device immediately completes it.
- [ ] Pause and archive while another account submits a check-in.

## Final retry and offline replay

- [ ] Rapidly tap the same check-in action more than once.
- [ ] Enable airplane mode immediately after submit, then reconnect.
- [ ] Background and force-close the app during an uncertain submission.
- [ ] Reopen the app and confirm the authoritative participant and rollup state.
- [ ] Remove membership, assignment, or manager authority before replaying a queued action.

## Final side-effect and queue acceptance

- [ ] Observe exactly one activity, inbox item, and push for each eligible transition.
- [ ] Confirm actor, removed-member, and viewer suppression.
- [ ] Change group mute and Activity preference after queueing but before delivery.
- [ ] Confirm delayed delivery still opens the exact occurrence.
- [ ] Observe scanner retry after a transient failure or interrupted processing lease.
- [ ] Confirm a completed request is not sent again on a later scan.

All rows in these final sections remain pending until an explicitly approved
device/deployed-runtime acceptance pass.
